Here is the exact 5-agent breakdown & setup architecture:

1. Company Pulse Agent
Synthesizes Slack messages, CRM updates, and Stripe financial data. Output: A 2-minute daily founder brief containing team blockers, unhappy clients, and pipeline momentum.

2. Churn & Revenue Risk Agent
Monitors customer health signals, usage drop-offs, and unresolved support tickets. Output: Alerts account managers 30 days BEFORE a client cancels.

3. Hiring & Interview Prepper Agent
Reads candidate resumes & HR notes. Output: Generates a 10-point founder-fit scorecard and exact deal-closing interview questions before every final round.

4. Calendar & Priority Guard Agent
Sorts daily priorities, color-codes time blocks, and protects deep work hours from unnecessary meeting invites.

5. Sales Performance & Pipeline Agent
Audits rep outreach, tracks deal velocities, and triggers automated WhatsApp/email follow-ups for stalled proposals.
