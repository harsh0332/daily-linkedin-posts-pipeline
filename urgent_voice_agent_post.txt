Here is the complete step-by-step master breakdown of AI Calling Agents—PSTN, SIP Trunks, the 4-step voice pipeline, open-source frameworks, and real cost economics:

1. Telephony Basics: PSTN vs SIP Trunks
- PSTN: Traditional mobile/landline networks.
- SIP Trunk: The virtual pipe connecting your internet AI server to mobile carriers (Exotel, Vobiz for India +91 TRAI DLT compliance; Twilio/Plivo for global DIDs).

2. The 4-Step Technical Voice Pipeline (<800ms Latency)
- VAD (Voice Activity Detection): Detects speech vs silence.
- STT (Speech-to-Text): Deepgram or Sarvam AI (for Hindi/Hinglish).
- LLM (Language Model): GPT-4o or Claude 3.5 Sonnet for intent & context.
- TTS (Text-to-Speech): ElevenLabs or Sarvam TTS.

3. No-Code Platforms vs Open-Source Frameworks
- No-Code (Retell AI, Vapi, Synthflow, Bolna AI): Quick 30-min setup, but costs ₹14–₹15/minute.
- Open-Source Coding Route (LiveKit Agents, Pipecat): Full code ownership, WebRTC streaming, and zero platform markups.

4. The Real Cost Breakdown (10,000 Call Minutes/Month)
- No-Code Route: ~₹1,50,000 / month (at ₹15/min).
- Custom Coding Route (Sarvam/Deepgram + VPS): ~₹35,000 / month (at ₹3.5/min) + ₹500 VPS hosting.
